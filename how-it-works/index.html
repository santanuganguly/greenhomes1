Construction Process

From plot survey to key handover

Six structured milestones with locked per-sqft pricing, contractual completion dates, and single-point engineering accountability.

Stage 01 · Requirement
01

Share your requirement

20-minute call or a quick form. We listen first, no sales pitch. You tell us about your plot, your family, your must-haves, and your timeline. We tell you honestly if we're a fit.

  • 20-minute discovery call or form
  • Plot, family & timeline review
  • Honest fit & feasibility assessment
Stage 02 · Design
02

Site visit & design

Our architect visits the plot, measures, and prepares concept drawings and 2D/3D plans. Two rounds of revisions are included before we lock the design.

  • On-site plot inspection & measurements
  • Concept drawings & 2D/3D floor plans
  • Two revision rounds included
Stage 03 · Contract
03

Fixed-price contract

You receive a per-sqft quote with a complete bill of materials, a milestone payment schedule, and a guaranteed handover date. Your dedicated project manager is named in the contract.

  • Per-sqft quote with complete bill of materials
  • Milestone-based payment schedule
  • Named project manager & handover date
Stage 04 · Construction
04

Construction

Daily site photos and weekly quality-check reports land in your dashboard. Material deliveries are photographed on arrival. Milestone payments release only after you sign off on each phase.

  • Daily site photos & weekly quality reports
  • Delivery verification photographs
  • Payments release only after your sign-off
Stage 05 · Audit
05

Independent quality audit

Before handover, an independent auditor runs 470+ quality checks: concrete, steel, plumbing pressure, electrical, waterproofing, finishes. Snags are fixed at our cost, not yours.

  • 470+ independent quality check points
  • Concrete, plumbing, electrical & waterproofing
  • All snags rectified at our cost
Stage 06 · Handover
06

Move in & warranty

Keys, appliance manuals, finish schedules, and warranty cards. A comprehensive 5-year warranty, and a direct phone line to your PM for the first year.

  • Keys, appliance manuals & finish schedules
  • Comprehensive 5-year warranty
  • Direct phone line to your PM for year one
